## 📊 Sector Overview & Market Dynamics

The global **SD-WAN and Network Traffic Aggregation** market is valued at approximately **$5.8B – $6.5B** entering 2025–2026, projected to expand at an 18–22% CAGR as distributed enterprises migrate from costly MPLS backbones to agile SASE and cloud-delivered software-defined WAN fabrics. The commercial market is **moderately concentrated** among established networking and cybersecurity conglomerates (Cisco, Palo Alto Networks, Fortinet, HPE Aruba, Broadcom/VMware, and Cloudflare) capturing high-capital enterprise contracts, while innovative edge providers (Cato Networks, Versa, Aryaka) and a vibrant open-source ecosystem (featuring DPDK, MPTCP, and WireGuard bonding) drive high fragmentation and agility for homelabs, branch offices, and edge computing. 🌐
